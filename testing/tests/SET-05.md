## Why

The tuners and checks are made for a student in a first season with no Java. Each screen must say what
moves, how to stop it, the one thing to do now, and the exact lines to change. A screen that confuses a new
student is a bug, even when the numbers are right.

## Safety

- Eye protection. Robot on a block for every tool that spins a wheel or a flywheel.
- Keep a person ready on STOP. Nobody in front of a launcher.

## Setup

- Your robot in five files, with Tune and check in the TeleOp list.
- A student who did not write the robot's code and did not read the docs. A mentor watches and does not help.

## Steps

1. The student opens Tune and check and runs the Hardware Check, then one more tool for each part the robot has.
2. For each tool, before ▶, the student says out loud what will move and how to stop it.
3. The student holds a stick or a button while pressing ▶ or picking a device. Nothing may move.
4. The student does each tool to its end from the screen alone. Open help (GUIDE, or hold START 1 s) only when stuck.
5. At each end screen, the student says DONE or NOT DONE, and which file, class and lines to change.
6. Press STOP in the middle of one moving test. Read the STOPPED screen.

## Pass rule

In every tool, step 2 names the moving part and the stop, step 3 moves nothing, step 5 names the right file,
class and lines, and step 6 stops the motion at once. The mentor helped 0 times.

## Record

- the robot, the library version, the student's grade and season
- each tool run, and every word or line the student asked about (copy the screen line)
- each time the mentor had to help, and why

## If your robot is different

Run only the tools for the parts your robot has. The menu lists only those.
